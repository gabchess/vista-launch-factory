## Draft

### The next move on a live deal shouldn’t be a vibe

You already have an opportunity. Notes. A few emails. Maybe a CRM export. What you don’t have is a clean ruling on what the deal deserves next.

So the room debates. Someone pushes to advance. Someone wants another meeting. Someone quietly hopes it dies. The conversation is loud. The evidence trail is not.

**Quorum Desk** is a buyer-decision Augment for that moment. It helps you decide the next move on a live B2B opportunity: advance, validate, recover, hold, disqualify, or exit. (qd1)

It does not pretend every sentence in your notes is a fact. Claims stay tagged — observed, buyer-stated, inferred, assumed, unknown, disputed, or disconfirmed — so inference doesn’t get laundered into certainty. (qd2)

And it does not send mail, update CRM, or make commercial promises for you. Humans still approve outreach and system changes; Quorum Desk analyzes and recommends. (qd3)

### What shipped in v0.1.0

Install doors are dual-host by design: a Codex skill folder and a Claude ZIP, with parity intent. (qd4)

Start with one ask: *Tell me what opportunity we are actually in.*

### What this is not

Not a win-probability score. Not a prospecting engine. Not a silent sender. Not a claim that Vista Social pricing or seats changed — this fixture campaign is about Quorum Desk’s ship, labelled as rehearsal material until a real Vista release folder replaces it.
