# Keep the post with the approval task

**Preheader:** See the social content where the decision happens.

Your next approval should begin with the post in view.

Your approval queue has a task that says “Approve the next post.”

Which one? The version from this morning, or the one still sitting in drafts? Has someone already commented on it somewhere else? Did anyone already sign off, or is that still pending?

None of that lives in the task. Before you can approve anything, you go find the actual post, check who’s weighed in, then figure out what stage it’s at. When the task only says “Approve the next post,” finding the real post and context can take longer than the decision itself.

In Vista Work, the task and social posts are linked. Open the task and you see the actual post content, not a card describing it. Approvers can see what needs a decision instead of hunting for the post first.
