# Review your next post inside Vista Work

**Preheader:** Open the task and see the social content that needs a decision.

Your Vista Work task can keep the next social review in one place.

Your approval queue has a task that says “Approve the next post.”

Which one? The version from this morning, or the one still sitting in drafts? Has someone already commented somewhere else? Did anyone already sign off, or is that still pending?

None of that lives in the task. Before you can approve, you go find the actual post, check who’s weighed in, then figure out the stage. When the task only says “Approve the next post,” that hunt can take longer than the decision itself.

In Vista Work, the task and social posts are linked. Open the task and you see the actual post content, not a card describing it. Approvers can see what needs a decision instead of hunting for the post first.
