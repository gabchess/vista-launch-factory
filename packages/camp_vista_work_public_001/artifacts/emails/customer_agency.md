# Move client reviews into Vista Work

**Preheader:** Give approvers the post and task context together.

Your client review queue works better when each task carries its social post.

Your approval queue has a task that says “Approve the next post.”

Which one? This morning’s version, or the one still in drafts? Has the client already commented somewhere else? Did someone already sign off, or is that still pending?

None of that lives in the task. Before your approvers can decide, someone goes hunting for the real post, checks who’s weighed in, then figures out the stage. When the task only says “Approve the next post,” finding the real post and context can take longer than the decision itself.

In Vista Work, the task and social posts are linked. Open the task and the actual post content is right there, not a card describing it. Approvers can see what needs a decision instead of hunting for the post first.
