Give every client approver the post in context

Your team handles many client decisions. Each one needs the current post and its context.

Your approval queue has a task that says “Approve the next post.”

Which one? This morning’s version, or the one still in drafts? Has the client already commented somewhere else? Did someone already sign off, or is that still pending?

None of that lives in the task. Before anyone can approve, someone on your team goes hunting for the real post, checks who’s weighed in, then figures out the stage. When the task only says “Approve the next post,” that search can take longer than the decision itself.

In Vista Work, the task and social posts are linked. Open the task and the actual post content is right there, not a card describing it. Approvers can see what needs a decision instead of hunting for the post first.
