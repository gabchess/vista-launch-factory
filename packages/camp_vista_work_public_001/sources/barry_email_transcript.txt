Content summary:

- The email opens on the pain of an approval-queue item that names a post
  without showing it: the approver cannot tell which version it refers to,
  whether the client already commented elsewhere, or whether sign-off has
  already happened.
- It observes that none of that context lives in the task itself, so the
  approver goes hunting for the post, its feedback, and its stage before
  deciding. The search takes longer than the decision.
- Product fact: in Vista Work, tasks and social posts are linked. Opening
  the task shows the actual post content, not a card describing it, so
  approvers see what needs a decision instead of hunting for it first.
- It closes with a call to action to open Vista Work.
