The work behind your content finally has a home

Every social team runs the same split: content lives in Vista Social, but the work about that content lives somewhere else. A board in one tab, a composer in another, and you in the middle copying context between them.

Vista Work closes that gap. Plan, assign, and track the work behind every post, conversation, review, and campaign in the same tool where the posts live. One workspace. One login. One source of truth.

No more recreating tasks that already exist. No more explaining which client, which post, which conversation. The task knows because it carries a live link back to the social item that created it.

What just changed

  • The second tool is gone: The project board and the social platform are the same platform now. Your team stops paying twice, learning twice, and updating twice.
  • Tasks know what they’re about: Every task links to the post, inbox conversation, review, report, listener, or automation behind it. Click a task, land on the source. No hunting.
  • Work creates itself: An Instagram DM becomes a task through an automation. Inbox messages, listening matches, and macros spin up tasks without anyone copying and pasting. The composer can create a linked task for every post it publishes.
  • A cleaner personal queue: Open My Work to see today’s assignments, upcoming deadlines, and overdue work across every project and client.
  • It’s deliberately simple: Projects, tasks, boards, and calendars. No Gantt charts, no formula fields, no wiki sprawl. This is the antidote to PM tools your team dreads opening.

What you can do right now

My Work: one screen, every client

My Work brings your assignments together across every client, brand, and project. It separates work due today from upcoming and overdue, so an agency SMM can plan the day without opening each client board.

A community follow-up, a Reel approval, and a report can share one personal queue while staying inside their original projects.

Projects shaped around your team

Create a project for a client, brand, campaign, or internal team. One profile group can support several projects, so a large account can separate always-on content from a product launch or community work.

Each project gets its own statuses and custom fields. Add text, number, date, dropdown, checkbox, or link fields to track whatever matters to that client: market, campaign code, legal owner, final asset.

Three views of every project: Board (kanban), List, and Calendar. Save views the whole team shares, switch between them without losing context.

Tasks with enough context to act

Multiple assignees, priorities, labels, due dates with time and timezone, rich text descriptions, media library attachments, subtasks, checklists, comments with @mentions, and custom statuses per project.

Notifications respect your settings: assignment, mentions, due soon, and morning-after overdue reminders across email, in-app, browser, and Slack. Each teammate picks what works for them.

Create a task from anywhere: Shift+T

Press Shift+T on any screen in Vista Social. The task automatically links to whatever you’re looking at: an inbox conversation, a scheduled post, a review, a report, a Vista Page, a listening result, an automation, or advocacy content.

The assignee works from the current source rather than an old screenshot or rushed summary. A task created from a report preserves its selected filters, so the next person doesn’t rebuild the view.

The 30-second pitch

Open an inbox conversation. A customer asks for a spec sheet under your latest Reel. Create a task, assign the designer, set Friday as the due date.

The task carries a live link back to the original conversation. The designer opens it, reads the customer’s exact words, sees which profile received it, and delivers the asset. No Slack message asking, “Which post was this about?” No screenshot pasted into a card.

Then open My Work. Everything due today, across all your clients, on a single screen. No back-and-forth, no lost context, just the work that needs doing.

Work that creates itself

Some assignments should begin when a social event happens. Vista Work can create those tasks through automations, so repeatable follow-ups don’t depend on somebody copying them into a board.

An Instagram DM can trigger a task. Inbox messages, listening matches, and macros can also create work, while the composer can make a linked task for every post it publishes.Use those workflows to:

  • Assign sales-ready DMs to the teammate handling social leads.
  • Send sensitive comments into a review queue with a due date.
  • Turn relevant listening matches into community or PR follow-ups.
  • Create a post-publish check for content that needs close monitoring.

Start with one event and choose a clear owner, then check the first few tasks before expanding the automation.

AI belongs here too

Ask Vista and your connected AI tools can read, draft, and organize tasks right alongside your team. Let AI handle the administrative heavy lifting, such as triaging inbox requests or tagging priority items. All this happens while your human team keeps full control over approvals and strategy.

The pattern is simple: AI handles task administration, humans decide what matters. An agent might prepare the day’s queue, but the social team still decides what deserves attention and what reaches the public.

For agencies: drop your second PM tool

You’re paying per seat for a project board, then paying per seat for Vista Social, then spending your team’s hours keeping them in sync. Every client is two sets of tasks, two sets of statuses, two places to check.

Vista Work is included. Every teammate who can post can also manage work. Same clients, same profile groups, same permissions. One vendor, one invoice, one place to train new hires.

Set up each client with its own project, custom statuses that match their approval flow, and fields that track what matters to them. The process stays consistent where it should and flexible where it needs to be.

For more ways to structure client access and workflows, explore Vista Social for social agencies.

Why this isn’t just another project board

General project tools handle owners, dates, dependencies, and cross-department planning well. Keep them for company launches, staffing, budgets, and projects that extend far beyond social.

Those platforms don’t own your social data, so a task only carries information about a post, DM, inbox conversation, or approval when somebody adds it. That extra coordination matters when workflow and approval problems are already common. A benchmark study found that 33% of B2B marketers considered managing workflow issues or content approvals a challenge.

Vista Work keeps the social source attached to the task. The assignee can see what started the work, open the current post or conversation, and act with the original context in front of them.

Use Vista Work for the social part of your operation. When an assignment begins with a post, message, review, idea, report, listening result, or automation, the task can stay beside its source.
