# Product

## Identity

Sup, I’m Tix. Your garage sale flipper.

Tix helps secondhand buyers and sellers make the next decision. A buyer can
describe an item, location and limit. A seller can bring a listing and ask for
help with the offer. The product starts as a conversation so the user does not
have to learn a parser or fill a dashboard before Tix understands the job.

## Current preview

The preview starts with a clean account. Google sign-in establishes identity.
The account owns its conversations, saved facts and preview records. Vercel AI
Gateway supplies the language model.

Marketplace search, listing retrieval, connector delivery, wallet funding and
checkout are simulated services. They produce stored records from the user’s
own item and location. The interface labels those records as simulated. It does
not present synthetic listings or wallet actions as live external events.

The price comparison is deterministic. Tix compares the asking price in a
record with the user’s limit. Market value remains unknown until the product
has a supported evidence source.

## Release contract

The recording build runs as a restricted preview. After recording, the public
home page switches to a waitlist. Invited accounts keep access through a
server-owned entitlement. The same entitlement is required during recording.
The browser cannot grant itself access.

## Product question

The first question is whether repeat buyers will return to Tix with another
item or listing. The launch video shows the intended workflow. It does not
count as user demand, marketplace coverage or purchase evidence.

## Payment direction

Tixmancer has separate Base Sepolia evidence for an x402 payment through its
own demo seller. A consumer wallet is optional. The planned flow gives the user
a scoped spending permission with an amount cap, expiry, approval and
revocation. Deposits and purchases stay outside the consumer preview until a
supported seller and the full permission-to-receipt path are verified.

## Next evidence

1. Verify live CDP Google sign-in and AI Gateway conversation on a preview.
2. Let people use Tix with their own items and locations.
3. Measure whether they return, save a hunt or ask to connect a notification
   channel.
4. Add one supported source and one useful notification connector.
5. Prove a capped purchase with one supported seller before widening wallet
   access.

## Operating constraints

Base Sepolia only. No custody. No marketplace scraping or vendor-site browser
automation. Account data and actions require server-verified ownership.
Historical evidence remains unchanged.
