About this demo ↗
What I do.
Where I’m headed.
I want to help you find overlooked garage items and snipe them below market value. Here’s what works today and what I’m building toward.
Available today
Your price rule, checked.
I save your hunt and check imported alerts against your limit. You decide what to open, pass or mark for contact.
Clear price checks
I show whether the asking price is under, at or over your limit. Market value stays unknown.
Decisions you can return to
I keep your hunt and decisions after refresh. Mark contact saves your intention; I don’t send a message.
On my roadmap
A closer look at the right finds.
Supported discovery
I’m working toward finding relevant listings through supported sources. Automatic discovery is planned.
Market-value evidence
I’ll need verifiable comparisons to estimate what an item is worth. Today, I check the asking price against your limit.
Supported seller checkout
I plan to connect supported sellers to checkout. You’ll set the spending cap and expiry, then approve the purchase.
Separate payment proof
Base Sepolia ticket demo.
My earlier ticket demo recorded a 5.00 test USDC payment through my own demo seller, after owner approval. Marketplace checkout still needs a seller integration.
