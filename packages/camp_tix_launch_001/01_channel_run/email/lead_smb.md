# A used camera hunt can start with one message

Preheader: Meet Tix, a conversational approach to secondhand buying. Start with the item you need and the amount you can spend.

Hi there,

Your next product shoot needs a camera. You have a budget in mind, and a few minutes between everything else that needs doing.

We're building Tix to start that secondhand hunt with a conversation. Tell it what you're looking for and where to look. Add the most you're willing to spend.

For example: “I'm in Brooklyn, looking for a used Sony camera for less than $950.” That gives the conversation a useful starting point. In the preview, Tix compares an item's asking price with the limit you've chosen.

The walkthrough shows how the conversation is designed to work. Live marketplace sources are part of the next stage. Join the waitlist to follow what we're building.
