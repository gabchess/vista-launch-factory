Before the next secondhand buy, set your limit

Hi there,

When you're sizing up a secondhand item, the asking price is only part of the decision. You also need to know the most you're willing to pay.

Tix starts with that context. The product is being built for secondhand buyers and sellers, with a conversation about an item or listing. In the preview, a price comparison uses the buyer's chosen limit. Market value still needs its own evidence.

The camera walkthrough gives you a concrete way to understand the idea: a buyer in Brooklyn wants a used Sony for less than $950. It follows the intended experience from that first request.
