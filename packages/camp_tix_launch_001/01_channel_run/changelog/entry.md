# Tix preview: start your secondhand hunt with a conversation

Tix is taking shape around a simple starting point: tell it what you're looking for. The preview shows how a secondhand hunt can begin with a conversation about the item you want and where you live.

Add the most you're willing to spend. Tix compares the asking price in a preview record with your chosen limit, so you can see how it fits your budget. Market value needs separate evidence.

The walkthrough follows a buyer looking for a used Sony camera in Brooklyn for less than $950. Use that example to think through your own first request, or start with a listing you'd like to discuss.

Marketplace and wallet steps in the current preview use stored simulations. Supported marketplace sources and user-selected notifications are planned next.
